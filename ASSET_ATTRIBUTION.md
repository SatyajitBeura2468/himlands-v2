# Environment Material Attribution

The high-resolution environment material maps in `src/assets/world/` are CC0 assets from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/).

- `aerial_rocks_01` — rock albedo, normal, and roughness maps
- `dark_wood` — timber albedo, normal, and roughness maps
- `bark_brown_02` — tree bark albedo, normal, and roughness maps
- `aerial_grass_rock` — alpine ground, grass, and shrub albedo, normal, and roughness maps

Poly Haven distributes its assets under CC0. The files are used as part of the custom HIMLANDS WebGPU material pipeline.
