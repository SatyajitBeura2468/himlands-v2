# Audio Sources

Himlands uses the following CC0 (public-domain dedication) sound effects from
OpenGameArt. They are retained locally in `src/assets/audio/` and are used only
as interactive sound effects; no music is included.

| Local asset | Source | Creator / uploader | Licence | Use in Himlands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `snow-footstep-a.flac`, `snow-footstep-b.flac` | [4 dry snow steps](https://opengameart.org/content/4-dry-snow-steps) | Iwan “qubodup” Gabovitch | CC0 | Alternating compacted-snow boot crunches |
| `snow-surf-wind.ogg` | [wind whoosh loop](https://opengameart.org/content/wind-whoosh-loop) | SketchMan3 | CC0 | Snow-surf bed and Vortex gust |
| `ribbon-water-loop.ogg`, `ribbon-bubble.ogg`, `sweep-water.ogg`, `sweep-snow-spray.ogg`, `bloom-splash.ogg`, `bloom-impact.ogg`, `bloom-fallout-rain.ogg` | [40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx) | rubberduck | CC0 | Layered water and snow material for Ribbon, Sweep, Bloom and Vortex |
| `crystallize-ice.wav`, `crystallize-coldsnap.wav` | [Ice spells](https://opengameart.org/content/ice-spells) | bart | CC0 | Main ice split and delayed cold fracture for Crystallize |

The source pages label these files CC0; attribution is not required, but this
record preserves provenance for future Himlands development.
